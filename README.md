# Coraline's Survivors
Coraline's Survivors is a small survival game for the Windows console, made with C++. Coraline is trapped in a theater in  the Other World and flying dogs are after her. Shoot them with her slingshot, clear every room and escape.

![Snowglobe](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a18567-82fe-79df-a0bd-b68e84ebb64a/image.png)

## How to play
- Move with **W A S D**
- Shoot with **Space** (it fires where you are facing) or the **arrow keys**.
- Clear all the dogs in a room, then walk to the green door ('=') to move on. Clear the last room to win.
- Dogs drop a '*' when they die. Pick it up to get XP: every 5 XP you level up (+20 life and +1 damage).
- The slingshot slows down the dogs it this.
